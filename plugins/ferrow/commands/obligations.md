---
description: List open Ferrow obligations, or satisfy or waive one
argument-hint: "[satisfy N | waive N <reason>]"
---

The Ferrow mod answers this command itself. If you are reading this, the mod is
not running in this session. Say so in one line: `obligations_list` shows the
session's and an item's obligations, `obligation_satisfy` and
`obligation_waive` (with a reason of ten characters or more) answer them.
